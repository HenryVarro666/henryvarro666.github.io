DATA 602 team project (four people): joins 1,788 US state COVID-19 policy changes from OxCGRT to a per-state profile (census, vaccination, case peaks, hospital beds, party control) and to the CDC case trend on the day of each change, then fits decision trees, kernel ridge and k-means. Contribution: a Selenium scraper for data.census.gov state profiles, the obesity and fire-department data, and the clustering notebook. Bachelor's-degree share tracks mid-August 2021 vaccination (r = 0.67 across 49 states). The trend targets end on the change day, so the models describe the run-up to each policy rather than its effect, and the clustering notebook fits both of its k-means runs on the demographic table.
