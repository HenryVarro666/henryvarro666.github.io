This note is for someone holding a PS5 DualSense Edge wireless controller for the first time. Read it top to bottom and you will have the controller connected to the console, know every button, and be able to get back to the home screen. After that come the parts a standard PS5 controller does not have: swappable stick caps and back buttons, adjustable trigger travel, and profiles you switch with the Fn button.

Every step follows Sony's English support pages on playstation.com, and the text in quotation marks is Sony's own wording. Each section links its source at the top, and the full list is at the end. I checked the pages on 2026-11-28. Menu names can change after a system update, so go by what your screen shows.

## 1. What's in the box {#in-the-box}

The [DualSense Edge product page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/accessories/dualsense-edge-wireless-controller/) lists:

| Item | Count | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| DualSense Edge wireless controller | 1 | The controller |
| USB braided cable | 1 | Connects to the console and charges the controller |
| Stick caps: standard, high dome, low dome | 2 each | Swap them to suit your grip, see [section 6](#parts) |
| Half dome back buttons | 2 | Fit on the back of the controller, see [section 6](#parts) |
| Lever back buttons | 2 | Same, one left and one right; they are not interchangeable |
| Connector housing | 1 | Fits over the cable's plug so the cable does not pull out by accident |
| Carrying case | 1 | Holds the controller and parts; the controller can charge inside it |

## 2. The buttons {#buttons}

![DualSense Edge front view: the PS button is below the touch pad, between the two sticks; the create and options buttons are on either side of the touch pad; the d-pad is on the left and the action buttons on the right; the two Fn buttons are below the sticks.](front.svg "Figure 1: the front. The PS button is highlighted; the dashed outlines are the back buttons, which you cannot see from the front.")

The "Sony's description" column comes from [PS5 button functions](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/ps5-button-functions/); the Fn row comes from the [profile setup page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/set-up-edge-controller/).

| Button | Sony's description | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| PS button | Press once to access the control center. Press and hold to go to the home screen | How you get back to the home screen |
| Cross ✕ | Select the highlighted item | "OK" |
| Circle ○ | Cancel a command | "Back" |
| Directional buttons (d-pad) | Highlight an item on the screen | Moves the cursor; the left stick works too |
| Options button | Displays the options menu | You need it to close a game |
| Create button | Display the create menu and take screenshots and video clips | Screenshots and recordings |
| Mute button | Mutes your controller mic and speaker | The small bar below the PS button |
| Touch pad | Use for gameplay functions | Rarely needed outside games |
| Fn buttons (Edge only) | Hold Fn and press an action button to switch profiles; hold Fn and press the options button to return to the customization menu | Not used for going home; do not confuse them with the PS button, see [section 7](#profiles) |

Sony's pages call the PS5's main menu the home screen; "going home" in this note means going there.

![DualSense Edge back view: the two back buttons are on the back of the grips, and the L2 and R2 stop sliders are on the back below the triggers.](back.svg "Figure 2: the back. Seen from behind, left and right are swapped.")

Two more things on the back are not in the drawing: the RELEASE latch, used when you replace a stick module ([section 9](#stick-module)), and the small hole with the reset button, used to reset the controller ([section 11](#troubleshoot)). Sony's pages say only that both are on the back, without an exact position; turn the controller over and you will find them.

## 3. Connecting to the console the first time {#connect}

From [How to connect a DualSense Edge wireless controller](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/pair-edge-controller/):

1. Turn on your PS5 console.
2. Connect the controller to the console with the included USB cable.
3. Press the PS button. The controller turns on; after the light bar blinks, the player indicator lights up and you are connected.

Once the battery has enough charge you can unplug the cable and play wirelessly. Up to four controllers can be used at the same time, and when you press the PS button the console assigns each player a color.

**Fitting the connector housing.** In Sony's words, "to make sure the cable isn't accidentally disconnected, set up the connector housing":

1. Slide the latch on the connector housing to open it.
2. Put the USB Type-C connector into the housing and close the lid.
3. Check that the USB Type-C connector sits in the right position.

## 4. Charging {#charge}

- While the console is on or in rest mode, connect the controller to it with the included USB braided cable and it charges.
- In rest mode the light bar slowly blinks orange; when charging is complete, the light bar turns off.
- You can also charge from a computer or another USB device. Use a cable that complies with USB standards; Sony notes some devices may not charge the controller.
- The controller can charge inside the carrying case: open the flap on the back of the case to reach the USB port.
- To see how much battery is left, press the PS button once to open the control center and select Accessories.

## 5. Going home, the control center, and closing a game {#home}

Hold the PS button to go to the home screen; press it once to open the control center.

![Two ways from a game back to the home screen: hold the PS button to go straight there, or press it once to open the control center, pick Home and press the cross button.](home-flow.svg "Figure 3: two ways back to the home screen. The dashed line is the way back from the home screen to your game.")

**Way 1: hold the PS button.** In a game or app, hold the PS button and let go when the home screen appears.

**Way 2: through the control center.**

1. Press the PS button once. A row of icons appears along the bottom of the screen: that is the control center.
2. Move to Home with the d-pad or the left stick.
3. Press the cross button ✕ to go to the home screen. To change your mind, press the circle button ○.

**Back to your game.** Going home does not close the game. Sony says "some games and apps are paused when you display the home screen." Select the game on the home screen and press ✕ to carry on.

**Closing a game.** Press the PS button once, select Switcher, highlight the game you want to close, press the options button, and choose close in the menu that appears. Sony's page says only that the switcher lets you "close a game you're playing", so go by the wording on your screen. Closing a game without saving loses your progress, so save first.

What each control center icon does, in the order [How to customize the PS5 console control center](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/customize-ps5-control-center/) lists them. You can move the icons around, so the order on your screen may differ:

| Icon | Sony's description |
|---|---|
| Home | Displays the home screen |
| Switcher | Displays recently started games and apps. Switch between your games and apps or close a game you're playing |
| Notifications | Check notifications you've received |
| Game Base | Stay connected with your friends by viewing their activity, chatting via messages or voice, and seeing which console or device they're on |
| Music | Listen to music while you're playing a game |
| Sound | Manage your sound settings |
| Mic | Change your input device, mute and unmute your mic, and adjust the mic level |
| Accessories | Check your controller or headset battery level, and change their settings |
| Profile | Update your online status, access your profile, view your trophies, switch users, and log out |
| Power | Turn off your PS5 console or put it in rest mode |

## 6. Stick caps, back buttons and trigger travel {#parts}

From [How to personalize your DualSense Edge wireless controller](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/personalize-edge-controller/).

**Swapping stick caps** (standard, high dome and low dome):

1. Pull up on the stick cap on the controller and it lifts away.
2. Line up the cap you chose with the stick and push down until it is secure.

**Fitting back buttons:**

- The half dome back buttons fit either side; push them into the back button slots.
- The lever back buttons are not interchangeable. Check whether a button is marked R (right) or L (left), then push it into the matching slot.
- To remove one, pull it towards the top of the controller and lift it out of the slot.

What a back button does when you press it is set in a profile's button assignments, see [section 7](#profiles).

**Setting L2/R2 travel:** the L2 and R2 stop sliders on the back of the controller have three positions, long, medium and short stroke. Sony warns: "If you set the stop sliders to medium or shallow strokes, the trigger effect function doesn't work." The trigger effect is the resistance some games put into L2 and R2; Sony's example is drawing a bow or braking hard. To keep that feel, use the long stroke.

## 7. Profiles and the Fn button {#profiles}

A profile is a full set of button and feel settings. You can keep several and switch between them with the Fn button when you change games. From [How to set profiles for your DualSense Edge wireless controller on PS5 consoles](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/set-up-edge-controller/).

![Hold the Fn button and press the triangle button to switch back to the default profile; press the circle, cross or square button instead to switch to the profile assigned to it.](profiles.svg "Figure 4: Fn shortcuts. Fn + triangle is always the default profile.")

**Creating a profile:**

1. Go to Settings > Accessories.
2. Select DualSense Edge Wireless Controller > Custom Profiles.
3. Select Create Custom Profile, enter a name, then select OK.
4. Pick what to customize: Button Assignments, Stick Sensitivity/Deadzone, Trigger Deadzone, Vibration Intensity, Trigger Effect Intensity.

**Assigning it to an Fn shortcut:**

1. Go to Settings > Accessories.
2. Select DualSense Edge Wireless Controller > Custom Profiles.
3. On the Unassigned Profiles tab, select Assign next to the profile you want.
4. Select a shortcut. If that shortcut already has a profile, the new one overwrites it.

**Switching:** during a game, hold the Fn button and press the triangle, circle, cross or square button. Sony says "the profile assigned to the Fn button and the triangle button shortcut is the default profile and can't be changed", so if the buttons suddenly feel wrong, hold Fn and press triangle to get the defaults back.

**Adjusting mid-game:** hold the Fn button and press the options button to return to the customization menu.

A few limits:

- The Fn button cannot be given another function, and no other button can take over the Fn button's function.
- L2 and R2 can be swapped, but no other button can be assigned to work as a trigger.
- The controller stores up to four profiles, including the default one, and up to three can be assigned to shortcuts. This is on the [PC profile setup page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/set-up-edge-pc/).

## 8. Updating the controller software {#update}

On the PS5 (from the update part of the [connection page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/pair-edge-controller/)):

1. Go to Settings > Accessories > DualSense Edge Wireless Controller.
2. Select DualSense Edge Wireless Controller Device Software to update the controller.

Sony recommends keeping both the console's system software and the controller's device software up to date.

On a Windows PC: install the PlayStation Accessories app and open Firmware for your controller; Update appears when a new version is available. Sony warns not to turn off the PC or disconnect the controller during the update.

## 9. A stick stops working: replacing the stick module {#stick-module}

When a stick misbehaves or stops working, you can replace just the stick module. New modules are sold separately, and left and right modules are interchangeable, so one fits either side. From [How to replace a stick module](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/edge-replace-stick/):

1. Make sure the player indicator on the controller is off. If it is on, hold the PS button until it turns off.
2. Disconnect the USB cable from the controller.
3. Slide the RELEASE latch on the back of the controller and remove the front cover.
4. Raise the release lever.
5. Remove the old module and fit the new one. Check it is positioned correctly, then slide it along the grooves until you hear a click.
6. Lower the release lever.
7. Put the front cover back: line up its raised parts with the notches on the controller and push it carefully into place until it clicks.

## 10. Using it with a computer or phone {#other-devices}

From [How to use DualSense Edge wireless controllers with Windows, Mac, Android, and iOS](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/dualsense-edge-other-devices/). Sony lists Windows 10 (64 bit), Windows 11, macOS Ventura 13.3, iOS 16.4, iPadOS 16.4 and tvOS 16.4, or later.

1. Make sure the light bar and player indicator are off. If they are on, hold the PS button until they turn off.
2. Hold the create button, and while holding it, hold the PS button until the light bar blinks.
3. Turn on Bluetooth on the device, choose to add a Bluetooth device, and pick the controller from the list.
4. When pairing is done, the light bar blinks and then the player indicator lights up.

On a computer, the Fn button only switches between profiles already stored on the controller. If you go back to the PS5 and the controller will not connect, plug it into the console with the USB cable and press the PS button, the same as in [section 3](#connect).

{{< details summary="More: one controller, several devices" >}}
Sony says "you can have up to four different pairings registered to one controller, and each pairing is assigned a slot." The four slots match the four action buttons: triangle is 1, circle 2, cross 3 and square 4.

- To pair into a particular slot: hold that action button and the PS button together for over 5 seconds, then finish with steps 3 and 4 above.
- To switch between paired devices: hold the matching action button and the PS button for about 3 seconds.

## 11. When something goes wrong {#troubleshoot}

**Nothing happens when you press the PS button.** The battery may be flat. Connect the controller to the console with the USB cable, let it charge for a while, then press the PS button again.

**The buttons do not behave as usual.** You may have touched Fn and switched profiles; hold Fn and press triangle to return to the default. Sony also suggests checking the game's own controller settings in case they clash with your profile.

**Resetting the controller.** From [How to troubleshoot DualSense Edge wireless controller issues](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/edge-troubleshoot/):

1. Turn off the PS5 console.
2. Disconnect the USB cable from the controller.
3. Find the small hole on the back of the controller; the reset button is inside. Press it with a pin or similar tool. Sony's page does not say how long to press it.
4. Connect the controller to the console with the included USB cable and press the PS button.

Sony says this reset "won't reconfigure your controller or affect pairing and profile settings", so it is safe to try. The PlayStation Accessories app on a PC also has Reset Your DualSense Edge Wireless Controller, but that one is a factory reset: in Sony's words, "all your profiles are deleted, all settings are reset, and your controller returns to factory settings." If you only want the controller to respond again, use the hole on the back.

**Still not working.** First update the console's system software and the controller's device software ([section 8](#update)); if that does not help, go to [PlayStation Repairs](https://playstation.com/repairs).
